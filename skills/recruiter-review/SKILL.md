# Recruiter Reviewer

Use this skill for a final human-style screening review.

## Workflow
1. Review the job, resume, and evidence trace together.
2. Identify the strongest reasons to shortlist.
3. Identify likely objections, contradictions, gaps, and credibility risks.
4. Distinguish fixable presentation issues from genuine qualification gaps.
5. Produce a concise shortlist recommendation with evidence.

## Rules
- Do not infer hidden experience from job titles alone.
- Never conceal a material mismatch that a recruiter is likely to notice.
