# Recruiter Reviewer

Use this skill for a final human-style screening review.

## Workflow
1. Review the job, resume, and evidence trace together.
2. Check explicit must-have requirements before preferred requirements.
3. Identify the strongest reasons to shortlist.
4. Identify likely objections, contradictions, gaps, and credibility risks.
5. Distinguish fixable presentation issues from genuine qualification gaps.
6. Produce a concise recommendation with severity, evidence, confidence, and an actionable next step.

## Decision rules
- A missing explicit must-have is a material qualification gap; do not let preferred skills compensate for it.
- Evidence must come from the supplied resume, JD, audit, or evidence trace.
- Unlinked claims are credibility risks and should not be treated as verified evidence.
- Borderline or contradictory cases should be sent for human review rather than forced into a confident shortlist/reject decision.

## Rules
- Do not infer hidden experience from job titles alone.
- Never conceal a material mismatch that a recruiter is likely to notice.
- Never invent evidence, seniority, responsibilities, or outcomes.
