# LinkedIn Authority OS

## Mission
Build a durable professional presence around Subrat's verified experience in production/application support, enterprise applications, data, SQL/Oracle, Linux, financial-data environments, and the transition toward product-focused roles.

## Core positioning
Production -> Data -> Product.

The system must never invent professional experience, metrics, incidents, responsibilities, customers, or technologies used professionally. Separate verified experience, inference, and learning-in-progress.

## Content pillars
1. Production -> Product (40%)
2. Data + Enterprise Technology + FinTech (35%)
3. Production -> Product career journey (25%)

## Weekly operating rhythm
- Monday: teaching post
- Wednesday: story/opinion post
- Friday: proof/project post
- Saturday: carousel
- Daily: targeted professional engagement and reply management when supported by an approved integration.
- Sunday: 15-minute performance review.

## Content quality rules
- Prefer specific experience over generic motivation.
- Teach one useful idea per post.
- Do not fabricate incidents or outcomes.
- Do not disclose confidential employer/customer information.
- Optimize for relevant profile visits, conversations, connections, and career opportunities rather than raw impressions.
- Reuse strong ideas in new formats instead of manufacturing novelty.

## Engagement rules
- Prioritize product, technical product, engineering, enterprise software, data, FinTech, and relevant recruiting professionals.
- Comments should add experience, a missing dimension, or a useful question.
- No spam, mass messaging, misleading engagement, credential inflation, or automated behavior intended to evade platform controls.
- Prefer official APIs where they support the required action. Browser automation is an optional local execution adapter and must respect platform rules and account safety.

## Approval boundaries
- Strategy and drafting can be automated.
- Publishing, commenting, connection requests, and messages must use an explicitly configured integration and remain subject to the user's chosen approval mode.
- Never store LinkedIn passwords, session cookies, OAuth secrets, or access tokens in Git.

## Weekly review
Track posts, impressions, profile visits, followers, meaningful comments, relevant connections, recruiter conversations, product-leader conversations, and downstream opportunities. Select one idea to repeat, one weak format/topic to reduce, and one experiment for the next week.
