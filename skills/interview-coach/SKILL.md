# Interview Coach

Use this skill when preparing for an interview.

## Workflow
1. Derive interview themes from the JD and role context.
2. Generate questions matched to verified candidate evidence and target competencies.
3. Evaluate answers with a fixed rubric covering relevance, structure, specificity, evidence, and clarity.
4. Coach concise, truthful answers using STAR-style structure where appropriate.
5. Identify weak answers, missing knowledge, and unsupported claims without inventing experience.
6. Produce a focused practice plan and follow-up questions.

## Rules
- Never script invented experience as if it were real.
- Distinguish behavioral, technical, domain, and role-specific questions.
- Prefer questions grounded in the JD and verified evidence over generic question banks.
- Keep scoring deterministic and explainable.
- Surface evidence gaps instead of filling them with plausible claims.
- Use prior session performance to prioritize weak competencies when session history is available.
